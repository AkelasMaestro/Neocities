Example of an image

There are three main types of image files.

  • JPEG
  • GIF
  • PNG
JPEG's are usually used for images with a lot of detail, such as photographs. They use an algorithm to compress the image's file size at the cost of quality.
GIF's are typically used for images with solid colors, such as icons or logos. Gif's are also popularly used for motion or animation. They can only show 256 colors.
PNG's are typically used for versatile images in more complex designs, but are not always supported by older browsers. Both GIF's and PNG's allow for transparency.

For my own website, I'll need to experiment with what level of compression I am comfortable with.
