Feeling depressed? Make something. You'll feel better.

## What separates mankind from the rest of the animal kingdom?

Reiterate previous discussions with Dad on animals making tools, communicating etc.
Sliding goal post of what defines humanity.
Address pessimistic remarks on "humans are bad".

## Creation cuts both ways

We can create art and music and understanding. We can create destruction.
Creating destruction? You have to break eggs to make an omelette.
You have to tear down buildings to redevelop.
Destruction, removing the old, can be an important first step in creation.
But don't get stuck in that first step. Move forward and build up.

Also, don't be building with bad intent. You know what I mean.
What are you celebrating in your art? What is your message? Where is your focus?

## Creation is King

God made everything. We're made in God's image. We should make stuff.

When we create, we come closer to what makes us human.

You can make anything. This website for example. My model (link).

Just get started. It can be really bad and that's okay. You'll learn as you go.

Seriously, just start doing something.
