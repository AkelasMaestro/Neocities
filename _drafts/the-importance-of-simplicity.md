Websites, and computers in general, are over complicated.

My friend shared with me [this site](motherfuckingwebsite.com) and I loved it. Heads up: there is strong language. If that's not your thing, that's okay. I'll explain. It's a one-page website that explains what a "real" website should be, namely easy to read, focused on getting it's point across, accessible to the impaired, and not **wasting time** with fancy but useless design elements.

I really enjoy the bright and colorful neocity websites I come across around here. If you're coming from one of those, please do not take this call for simplicity as a criticism. Personalized websites that are obviously human designed and rough around the edges are why I came here in the first place. You bring color and vitality to what feels at times a dead internet. Do not change. My style is just different.

I like keeping things clean and tidy. To some extent, this website is meant to be an extension of my own mind, and I need that space to be clear. I'm also just starting out. I'm sure additional details and designs will come in the future.

### Not just Websites

One of my favorite YouTubers, [No Boilerplate] discusses the issue of simplicity in the context of software. Here's the [video] where it comes up. Computers are faster than they have ever been before, and yet it doesn't feel like it. Why? Because our ability to compute has been matched, and in some ways, out stripped, by our demand for more and more complicated programs.

It's my opinion that most people really don't need that much.
